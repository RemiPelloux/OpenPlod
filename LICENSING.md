# Licensing Scope

The [MIT license](LICENSE) applies to original OpenPlod contributions, including
its custom SVG icon set, original brand artwork, and documentation. It grants
rights only to material the named contributors are entitled to license.

## Inherited Code

OpenPlod derives from [jddavenportOpen/openplaud](https://github.com/jddavenportOpen/openplaud).
The upstream README declares **MIT**, but no standalone license file was
available when checked on September 7, 2026. Git history and upstream attribution
are preserved. This new license does not replace a missing upstream notice,
claim ownership of inherited code, or independently resolve that provenance gap.
Redistributors should confirm the inherited grant with its authors.

## Third-Party Material

Dependencies retain their own licenses and notices. Product photographs,
trademarks, and third-party assets are not covered by OpenPlod's MIT grant.
See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for source attribution,
asset terms, the historical proprietary Plaud SDK boundary, and distribution
prerequisites. No Plaud SDK binary, private device identity, account credential,
or recording is included in the public release.

OpenPlod is independent of Plaud and does not grant rights to Plaud's name,
logos, service, device firmware, or proprietary software.
